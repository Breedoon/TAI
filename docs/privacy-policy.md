# TAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-31

TAI ("we", "us") designs and sells graphic t-shirts. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or interact with our ads, and what we do with it.

## What we collect

- Information you give us when you order or contact us: name, shipping address, email, phone, and payment information (processed by our payment processor).
- Information collected automatically when you visit our site or see our ads: IP address, device and browser data, pages viewed, items added to cart, and similar usage data.
- Cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API, which send events such as page views and purchases from our site to Meta so we can measure and improve our ads.

## How we use it

- To fulfill orders and provide customer support.
- To send order updates and, if you opt in, marketing emails.
- To measure and improve our advertising, including building custom and lookalike audiences on Meta platforms.
- To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

## Who we share it with

- Service providers that help us run the business (payment processing, shipping, email, hosting, analytics).
- Advertising platforms, including Meta Platforms, Inc., for the purposes described above. Meta's use of that data is governed by Meta's own privacy policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy.
- Authorities where required by law.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

## Your choices

- You can unsubscribe from marketing email at any time using the link in any email.
- You can control how Meta uses your data in your Meta ad settings: https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences and https://www.facebook.com/off_facebook_activity.
- You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing us. Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/UK, California), you may have additional rights such as portability, objection, or opt-out of "sale" or "sharing"; we will honor these where applicable.

## Data retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law (for example, tax records). We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it. No system is perfectly secure.

## Children

Our site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will update the date above.
